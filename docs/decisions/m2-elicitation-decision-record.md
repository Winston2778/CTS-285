# M2 Elicitation Decision Record

## Investigation Path
1. What exactly do stakeholders mean by “students shouldn’t lose their work”?
   Evidence revealed: Teachers report that students may pause practice and return later. They want a learner’s saved practice state to remain available after leaving and returning to the application.
2. What do parents or teachers need to understand about learner activity?
   Evidence revealed: Adults want to understand what the learner practiced and whether progress is occurring, but stakeholders have not yet agreed on a detailed reporting dashboard.
3. Who will use the system and in what setting?
   Evidence revealed: The primary learner is a student practicing independently, often with a teacher or parent nearby. Exact device and access conditions have not yet been confirmed.

## Initial Position
**Supported evidence:**
Based on the information given, I feel as if DataMan should allow students to save their progress so they do not lose their work. DataMan should also provide clear and shorter instructions for adults so they can understand what the student is learning and the progress the student is making. If the student is practicing separately from an adult, the system may need to be improved to make sure the student can use it independently and that their progress can still be understood when they return.

**Remaining uncertainty:**

**Likely functional requirement:**

**Likely non-functional requirement / quality constraint:**

**Assumption or proposed solution I am not treating as confirmed:**

## Complication
Students may use DataMan on school Chromebooks, phones, tablets, and home computers. Some sessions may be interrupted before intentional sign-out.

**What this affects:**
Since students might use other devices that can do the same things as DataMan, their progress could be lost if it is not saved. DataMan should at least be able to save the student's progress so they can return later and continue their practice without losing their work.

**What I revised, if anything:**
would revise the requirement so students can save their DataMan progress using their student account or a flash drive. Since students may use different types of devices, they should not lose their progress when they switch devices or when a session is interrupted

**Final decision and reasoning:**
revise so that every student gets to save or access thier progress with either there school account or flash drive either way the progress will be saved and they can return to it
